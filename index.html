Physicl Scenes

An Artist-created 3D Dataset for Learning Perception and Action

Yueh-Cheng Liu Snehal Jauhri Matthias Nießner Angela Dai

Technical University of Munich

Paper Video Dataset
  • 6,499 scenes

    4,999 household rooms, 1,300 public interiors and 200 outdoor spaces.

  • 115K assets

    Across 1.6K categories, in over 560K instances.

  • Artist-created

    Every asset and scene is modeled by professional 3D artists. Nothing is procedurally generated.

  • Full PBR

    Albedo, roughness and metallic textures with explicit light emitters.

  • Part annotations

    Object- and part-level semantics.

  • Simulation-ready

    Metric scale, collision hulls, and mass and inertia derived from part materials.

Abstract

We present Physicl Scenes, a high-quality artist-created and curated dataset of 6,499 3D scenes spanning household rooms, public interiors, and outdoor spaces. The dataset combines detailed geometry, physically based materials, and carefully composed environments with object- and part-level semantic annotations, providing rich, joint supervision for visual and embodied learning. We demonstrate its value as training data across inverse rendering, 3D reconstruction, scene understanding, and mobile manipulation. Across these tasks, training state-of-the-art methods with our scenes improves performance over alternative synthetic datasets. In addition, we introduce a mobile manipulation benchmark, evaluating vision-language-action models performing physical pick and place in diverse 3D scenes with realistic layouts, and using our part annotations to also score where each object is grasped. Our dataset will be released publicly to facilitate research in visual and embodied AI.

Diverse scene types

Household rooms, public venues and outdoor spaces, each with a realistic layout, high-fidelity assets and rich lighting.

Mobile manipulation

Ground-truth mobile pick-and-place demonstrations for robot learning.

High-fidelity assets

Every asset is modeled by an artist and annotated with its parts.

Controllable Lighting and Full PBR Texture

Every scene places its own light sources over full PBR materials, so the same room renders at any time of day with any lamps on.
